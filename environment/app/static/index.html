GDPR Privacy & Telemetry Governance

Transparency Guarantee: This application captures active foreground application names and window titles strictly for focus scoring. No keyboard logging, screen scraping, or covert recording is executed.

Retention Policy: Telemetry records are stored locally in SQLite with WAL mode and automatically subject to a 30-day data retention cycle.

GDPR Article 15 (Right to Access): You can export full JSON activity logs at any time using the Export Telemetry button.

GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure): You can clear all recorded history using the Clear History action.
